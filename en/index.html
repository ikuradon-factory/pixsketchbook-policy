PixSketchbook Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ikuradon factory ("we") handles information in the Android app "PixSketchbook" (the "App").

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information or device information, including identifiers such as the advertising ID. The App does not send any information to us or to any third-party server.

The App does not connect to the internet, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

2. Your artwork and images

  • Artwork you draw is stored only in the App's storage on your device.
  • When you import an image, the App reads only the image you choose. Imported images are used only on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • When you save artwork as an image, it is saved to the photo folder on your device.
  • When you export your artwork (backup), the file is saved only to the location you choose.
  • When you share, the App uses Android's share feature and passes the image only to the app you choose. How that app handles it is governed by that app's own policy.

3. Device backup

If Android device backup is turned on, the App's data (such as your artwork) may be backed up to your Google Account. This is an Android feature provided by Google, and we cannot see the contents of these backups. You can turn this feature off in your device settings.

4. Ads and third-party services

The App contains no ads. It does not use any third-party services that collect information, such as analytics or advertising services.

When you choose to rate the App, the Google Play Store app opens. Google's Privacy Policy applies to information handled by Google Play.

5. Children

The App is not designed for children under 13. Because the App does not collect information from anyone, it does not collect information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will post the new version and its revision date on this page.
